Field book

Walk the site. Send the report.

Siteward turns photos, markup and your voice into a professional PDF report — punch lists, snag lists, site diaries, move-in/move-out inspections and site audits. On your iPhone, offline, no account.

How it works

1 · Photograph

Several photos per item, from the camera or your library, in the order you took them.

2 · Mark up

Circles, arrows, boxes, text and dimension lines on every photo. Edit them any time.

3 · Say it

“Kitchen, cabinet door misaligned, carpenter, high, due Friday.” The room, trade, priority, due date and assignee fill themselves in.

4 · Send

A branded PDF with a cover, summary table, annotated photos, checklist and signatures — by Mail or saved to Files. CSV too.

Templates

  • Punch list — final walkthrough before handover
  • Snagging list — new-build and handover snags
  • Site diary (Bautagebuch) — weather, crew and work done each day
  • État des lieux and vistoria de imóvel — room-by-room rental hand-overs with meters and keys
  • Move-in / move-out inspection — condition reports for landlords and tenants
  • Site audit — your own pass/fail checklist for safety, quality and housekeeping

Track every item to done

Open, in progress or fixed — with before and after photos side by side in the report. Drag items into the order you want; numbers follow.

Free and Pro

Every template, markup, dictation and PDF report is free, with one active project at a time and a small “Made with Siteward” line on reports. Siteward Pro adds unlimited projects, your logo and colour on every report, no footer line and CSV export — yearly with a free week, monthly, or a one-time lifetime unlock.

Help

The report won’t send by Mail. Mail needs an account set up in the Mail app. Use Share instead to send it with Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp or AirDrop, or Files to save it.

Dictation doesn’t start. Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for Siteward in Settings › Privacy & Security. You can always type, or use the keyboard’s mic.

Restore Pro on a new iPhone. Settings in the app › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Where is my data? On your iPhone only, and in your own encrypted iCloud Backup if you use it.
