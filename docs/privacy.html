Siteward

Privacy Policy

Updated 2 October 2026

In short: Siteward has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your projects, photos, notes and reports stay on your iPhone.

Projects, photos and reports

Projects, items, photos, markup, checklists, signatures, your company details and logo are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. If you use iCloud Backup they are part of your own encrypted device backup. The developer has no server and cannot see this data.

Camera, photos, microphone and speech

The camera and photo library are used only to add photos you choose to an item. When you dictate, your voice is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, on the device whenever your language supports it; for other languages Apple may process the audio under Apple's privacy policy. Filling in the fields from your note happens on the device, with Apple Intelligence where available. No recordings are kept.

Sharing

Reports and CSV files are created on your iPhone. They go only where you send them with Mail, the share sheet or Files.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app uses RevenueCat to confirm your Pro status. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (which product, when and its status) and a random anonymous app user ID generated on your device. This data is used only to provide purchases and restores. It is not linked to your identity, not used for tracking and not sold.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete an item or a project from its menu, or delete the app to remove everything. To ask about purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact the developer.
